LuzTech Blog is the official technical publication of **LuzTech Development**.

We use this space to share engineering articles, implementation notes, tutorials,
and lessons learned across the technologies we use to build products and internal
tooling.

## What you will find here

- Architecture and platform notes
- Frontend and backend tutorials
- Tooling, automation, and developer experience improvements
- Practical write-ups from the LuzTech engineering team

## Built with

This blog is powered by [Astro](https://astro.build/), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS,
Markdown/MDX content, Pagefind search, RSS, sitemap generation, and automatic Open Graph images.
