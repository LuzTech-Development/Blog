LuzTech Blog is the official technical publication of **LuzTech Development**.

We use this space to share engineering articles, implementation notes,
tutorials, architecture decisions, and practical lessons learned while
building products and internal tooling.

## What you will find here

- Frontend, backend, and platform tutorials
- Architecture and engineering notes
- Tooling and developer experience improvements
- Practical write-ups from the LuzTech engineering team

## Built with

This blog is powered by [Astro](https://astro.build/), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS,
Markdown/MDX content, Pagefind search, RSS, sitemap generation, and automatic
Open Graph images.
