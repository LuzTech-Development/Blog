Welcome to the official LuzTech technical blog.

This space exists to share practical knowledge from the LuzTech engineering team,
including tutorials, implementation notes, architecture decisions, and lessons
learned while building products and internal tooling.

The blog is powered by **Astro**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, and the
**AstroPaper** template foundation, with Markdown/MDX content, static search,
RSS, sitemap generation, syntax highlighting, and automatic Open Graph images.

We hope these articles help our team, partners, and the broader developer
community learn faster and build better software.
